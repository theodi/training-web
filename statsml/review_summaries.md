# Restaurant Review Summaries

## Four Peaks

### Food

**Positive Comments:**
- "Good food and good beer" - consistently praised for quality
- Beer battered fries are frequently mentioned as excellent, especially with jalapeño ranch sauce
- Loaded chicken nachos with large portion sizes
- Italian beer bread sandwiches highly recommended
- Carne Adovada receives rave reviews
- Fish and chips (battered in Kilt Lifter) described as "incredible" with crispy batter
- French Dip with thinly sliced roast beef praised
- Spinach artichoke dip called "best in the valley"
- Wings with fiery sauce
- Breakfast items (eggs benedict, braised pork dish) described as fantastic
- Pizza (Italian Classic) described as hot, fresh, and perfectly cooked when ordered well-done
- Chicken fingers, chicken salad pita, and various sandwiches consistently good
- Spicy Thai Hummus appetizer with a nice kick
- Sunday brunch highly recommended

**Mixed/Negative Comments:**
- One reviewer describes food as "bar food. Straight up bar food" - not exceptional
- Another reviewer notes food is "not super fantastic but it's better than a lot of brewery food out there"
- Burgers could use improvement according to one review
- Pizza described as "mediocre" by one reviewer
- Salads described as "pretty good"
- One reviewer notes they search for something they like enough to order again but haven't found it
- Food described as "generic yet palatable" by one critical reviewer
- One reviewer notes they don't go for the food, focusing on beer instead

**Overall Assessment:**
Food is generally well-received as quality bar/brewpub fare, with particular strengths in beer-battered items, sandwiches, and breakfast offerings. Portion sizes are noted as large. Some reviewers find it exceptional while others view it as good but not outstanding bar food.

---

### Drink

**Positive Comments:**
- Beer consistently described as "awesome," "great," "astounding," and "fantastic"
- Kilt Lifter (Scottish ale) frequently praised as smooth and thirst-quenching
- Hop Knot IPA and Raj IPA highly recommended
- Hopshot IPA described as great
- Pumpkin Porter receives exceptional praise, with customers purchasing growlers to take home
- Peach beer (Arizona Peach) described as legendary with low sweetness and crisp refreshing taste
- Hefe (Hefeweizen) praised, especially with lemon wedges
- Oatmeal Stout described as thick and hearty with no bitterness
- Seasonal beers (pumpkin porter, bourbon barreled porter, orange blossom, belgian white, barleywine) highly regarded
- Beer flight sampler recommended for first-timers (10 beers for around $9)
- Kolsch described as making one "swoon"
- 8th Street Ale recommended
- Cask conditioned ales mentioned as delicious (though often sold out)
- Cider available for non-beer drinkers
- Free beer samples available
- Beer described as authentic with attention to water chemistry matching Scottish/German water for authentic flavors

**Mixed Comments:**
- One reviewer notes Kilt Lifter is "a tad overrated, but still pretty solid"
- One reviewer found Raj IPA "heavy in diacetyls" but otherwise reasonable
- One reviewer notes they don't care for beer (reviewing from non-beer perspective)

**Pricing:**
- Happy hour daily from 3-7pm and 10pm-close
- Happy hour prices: $3 pints, $11 pitchers
- Reverse happy hour also available
- Growler pricing: $4 for growler, $10 for beer, $5 seasonal charge for seasonal brews
- Normal growler refills: $10

**Overall Assessment:**
Beer is the standout feature with overwhelmingly positive reviews. Wide variety of styles, seasonal offerings, and attention to brewing quality. Happy hour deals make it accessible. The brewery aspect is a major draw.

### Service

**Positive Comments:**
- "Great wait staff" and "great wait staff make this my favorite place"
- Waitresses described as "cute and friendly and not afraid to pour your drink"
- Service described as "fast and friendly even when the place is full to the brim"
- "Service was great as usual" even when "completely SLAMMED"
- "Service was exceptional"
- "Service was excellent"
- "Service has always been attentive"
- Bartender service praised for quick attention even in packed house
- Waiters described as "helpful and friendly and nice"
- Manager (Steve) specifically praised for being friendly and giving facility tours

**Mixed/Negative Comments:**
- "Sometimes the service is a little slow"
- One reviewer experienced "terrible service" - waitress took nearly 20 minutes to take orders and another 20 minutes before meals arrived
- Service described as "a little slow" by one reviewer
- Hostesses described as "extremely young and scantily clad" by one critical reviewer

**Overall Assessment:**
Service is generally well-regarded as friendly and efficient, even during busy periods. However, there are occasional reports of slow service, and one significant negative experience with very long wait times for ordering and food delivery.

---

### Cost

**Positive Comments:**
- "Prices are reasonable"
- "All of Four Peaks' menu items are appropriately priced"
- "The prices are very reasonable for specialty beer and good bar food"
- "Prices are reasonable and even better during happy hour"
- Happy hour: $3 pints, $11 pitchers (noted as "could be a little cheaper" but good value for daily happy hour)
- Beer sampler: 10 beers for around $9 described as great value
- "$3 locally brewed pints? Sign me up"

**Overall Assessment:**
Prices are consistently described as reasonable, with particular value noted during happy hour. The combination of quality beer and food at reasonable prices is frequently mentioned as a positive aspect.

## Matt's Big Breakfast

### Food

**Positive Comments:**
- Pancakes described as "best pancakes ever" - "fluffy little cakes of love" with secret ingredient
- Waffles consistently praised as "fluffy, light," "crisp," with "homemade flavor" - best waffles some reviewers have ever had
- Bacon frequently described as "awesome," "sweet, savory and cooked just right," "thick cut peppered bacon" - standout item
- Hash browns highly praised - "kick $ss hashbrowns," "amazing," "crispy," "cooked just right," "buttery"
- Home fries with rosemary described as "magnificent," "rocked"
- Toast and homemade jam/preserves frequently called the "best thing" or "show stealer" - strawberry jam, grape jelly, peach preserves
- Five Spot (egg sandwich) described as "best egg sandwich I have ever had" and "one of my favorite in the nation"
- Pork chop and eggs (hog and chick/chop and chick) highly recommended - "fabulous," "out of control," "delicious"
- Butter Burger praised - "Very, very, yummy," "Delicious"
- Eggs benedict described as "fantastic"
- Omelettes (ham and cheese, Portuguese sausage, cheddar jalapeño sausage) well-received
- Andouille sausage scramble described as "spicy but very good"
- Made from scratch food
- Local, organic ingredients frequently mentioned
- Real maple syrup (not artificial) appreciated
- Food described as "cooked to perfection with love and kindness"
- Portions described as large/filling

**Mixed/Negative Comments:**
- One reviewer found waffle "good but nothing memorable"
- One reviewer found hash browns "way too greasy to be enjoyable"
- One reviewer found bacon "overcooked"
- One reviewer found pork chop "under-seasoned"
- One reviewer found hash browns "crisp and tasty on the outside but soggy on the inside"
- One reviewer found salami "way too salty"
- One reviewer found hash browns "more like a chopped potato omelet that needed a few more minutes" - "raw mashed potato in the middle"
- Eggs described as "over easy" taken too seriously - "egg tar tar" - too runny to eat with fork
- One reviewer notes portions in pictures are "very deceptive" - waffle and pancakes "aren't big as they look"
- One reviewer found food "good breakfast food, not GREAT" - "over hyped"
- One reviewer found food "never rose above 'good'" except toast and jam
- One reviewer found food "below Waffle House" quality

**Overall Assessment:**
Food receives very strong reviews overall, with particular praise for waffles, pancakes, bacon, hash browns, and toast with jam. The use of local, organic ingredients and made-from-scratch preparation is frequently noted. However, there are some inconsistencies reported, particularly with egg preparation and occasional quality issues with specific items.

---

### Drink

**Positive Comments:**
- Coffee described as "special blend just for them" - local
- Lemonade made from scratch and sweetened with honey described as "awesome"
- Fresh squeezed orange juice available
- Free refills of ice tea mentioned

**Mixed/Negative Comments:**
- Coffee described as "average at best" by one reviewer
- Fresh squeezed orange juice described as "enough liquid to maybe fill up a double shot glass" - very small portion
- One reviewer notes restaurant "ran out of cream for coffee" with no apologies

**Overall Assessment:**
Drink offerings are limited (coffee, juice, lemonade, tea) as expected for a breakfast-focused restaurant. Coffee is local/special blend but receives mixed reviews. Lemonade made from scratch is well-received. Portion sizes for juice are noted as small.

### Service

**Positive Comments:**
- "Service was extremely fast once you got in the door and the waitstaff are very friendly"
- "Service was prompt and friendly"
- "Service was great" - quick meal delivery, coffee mugs never empty
- "Service was fast, and spot on"
- "Service was great" - no attitudes detected
- Owner personally helped explain seating and offered to call when table ready
- Waitresses "always serve us quickly and never leave us hanging, and they're always smiling"
- Innovative service: servers come outside to take orders while customers wait, food delivered quickly after seating
- "Great company and service"

**Mixed/Negative Comments:**
- "The service sucks at Matt's" - though later updated to improved
- "Service was a disappointment, server was very forgetful and put off by some simple requests"
- "I felt that the waitresses were attempting to rush us out of the restaurant" - "overt pushiness was not warranted"
- "The seating is cramped and the service is rushed"
- "No more snotty waitresses" (in update, suggesting previous issues)
- Service described as "rushed" by multiple reviewers

**Overall Assessment:**
Service receives mixed reviews. Many praise fast, friendly service, while others report feeling rushed or experiencing poor service. There are reports of improvement over time. The innovative approach of taking orders while customers wait is appreciated.

### Cost

**Specific Pricing Mentions:**
- One meal: $21 (Five Spot with homefries, bacon and waffles, OJ and coffee)
- Another meal: $22.53 + $4.00 tip (omelet, eggs benedict, home fries, coffee, ice tea)

**Positive Comments:**
- "Absolutely worth every penny"
- "Great value for the price" - one reviewer packed up half their meal
- "Reasonably priced for your basic breakfast"

**Negative Comments:**
- "Pricey for some dishes"
- One reviewer notes it's "reasonably priced" but "that is about it" - not exceptional value

**Overall Assessment:**
Pricing is generally described as reasonable for breakfast, with meals typically in the $20-25 range for two people. Some find it good value, while others note it can be pricey for some dishes. The quality of ingredients (local, organic) may justify the pricing for many customers.
